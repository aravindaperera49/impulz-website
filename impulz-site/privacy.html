LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

We value the trust you place in IMPULZ Design Studio. This policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website or work with us.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

IMPULZ Design Studio is a graphic design and creative services studio founded by Aravinda C. Perera and based in Sri Lanka. For questions about this policy or your personal information, contact us at impulzcreations@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive information you choose to share, including your name, business name, email address, telephone number, project requirements, brand assets, files, and communications. If you submit a testimonial, we may also collect the name, business or professional details, rating, and feedback you provide.

Our website and its hosting or security services may also process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and basic request or usage data as part of operating and protecting the website.

3. How we use information

We use information as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, discuss and deliver design services, communicate about projects, manage our business relationship, process agreed payments through third-party payment providers, maintain website functionality and security, and display testimonials where submitted and approved for publication. We may also retain relevant records for legitimate administrative, accounting, or legal purposes.

4. WhatsApp, email, and external services

When you contact us using WhatsApp or email, your communications are also handled by the relevant service provider under its own terms and privacy practices. If you follow a link to a third-party website or use an external payment service, that provider's privacy policy applies to information it collects. Please review those policies before sharing sensitive information.

5. Client testimonials

If you submit a testimonial through our website, the information you provide may be reviewed before publication. Approved testimonials may appear publicly on our website, including the display name, professional or business details, rating, and written review you supplied. Please avoid including confidential or sensitive information in a testimonial. To request a correction or removal, email us at impulzcreations@gmail.com.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to host and secure our website, communicate with clients, deliver projects, or facilitate payments. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect our rights and the security of our services.

7. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including ongoing client relationships, project administration, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no website, electronic communication, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary enquiry channels.

9. Your privacy choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to raise a concern about how it is used. We will review requests and respond in accordance with applicable law and any relevant recordkeeping obligations.

10. International services

As we work with clients internationally and use third-party online services, information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own. Where applicable, processing is subject to the practices and safeguards of the relevant providers and applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, services, or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please email impulzcreations@gmail.com.
